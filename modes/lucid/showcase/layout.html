Upstream's rubric, kept as it was written.
camera-elevation-and-framingcomposition

Seen more steeply than the target, exposing a broad upper landing and leaving the character smaller and higher in the frame.

Fix — lower the camera and aim it so the character sits in the lower central quarter and the steps read frontally; keep the cropped shrine crown.

stair-too-pristinematerials

Identical uninterrupted black risers with straight light edges and little wear.

Fix — vary tread corners and slab joints, chip the edges, sag and break sections, add puddles, moss and amber reflected streaks.

insufficient-foreground-occlusioncomposition

The foreground lamps and plinths take far less of the frame than in the target; the right column is a narrow small element.

Fix — bring the two front lamp pedestals closer and enlarge them; move the right column forward with a heavier shaft and base.
