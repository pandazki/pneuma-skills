Upstream's rubric, kept as it was written.
camera-framingcomposition

Steeper and taller than the target; it exposes the platform perimeter and an empty foreground strip.

Fix — use the target's ~16:9 frame, lower the camera, move the framing inward so the boundaries fall outside the edges.

reflection-charactermaterials

Reflections are smooth elongated patches on uniform paving; the target has irregular puddles, dry islands and fine glints.

Fix — puddle masks, multiscale normals, per-paver wetness; break reflections at raised edges.

character-silhouettedetails

Still a square-headed block figure with a broad flat cape and no readable sword.

Fix — refine helmet and shoulders, articulate the limbs, add a sword angled down, taper the cape into torn folds.
