# Prompt pack — lab-walk

## Who the pawn is
The white pawn-shaped figure is a placeholder for a real person: a scientist in a pale lab coat, mid-thirties, calm.

## Body action (from the beats)
- 0.5–3.8 s walks in from the right with a relaxed natural gait — real steps, arms swinging, never gliding — along the pawn's path
- 3.8–4.5 s slows and stops at the pedestal where the pawn stops, facing the device
- 4.5–5.5 s raises the left hand and presses the lit button on top of the pedestal
- 5.5–7.5 s stands, hand resting on the pedestal, watching the device

## Look
A clean near-future laboratory: brushed-metal equipment racks, soft cool overhead light, a polished dark floor with faint reflections. The box in front of the person is a slim control pedestal. The sphere and its rings are dull grey metal until the press.

## Camera
16:9. Slow push-in from low-left of the doorway, settling on a medium-wide frame with the whole device still in shot.

## Structure
One person, the device left of centre behind the pedestal, whole limbs, press before glow, one continuous shot.
