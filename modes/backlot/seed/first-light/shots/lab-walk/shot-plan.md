# Shot plan — lab-walk

**Idea.** A researcher walks into a quiet lab, presses a button on a console, and the device at the centre of the room wakes up blue.

## Layout
A 9 × 14 m room. Door in the south wall; equipment racks (0.9 × 1.6 × 2.2 m) along both side walls; a waist-high console (1.05 m) half a metre right of centre with a button on its top; the device — base, column, a 1.2 m sphere with three rings — 2.5 m behind the console. The researcher starts in the doorway and ends at arm's reach of the console, facing the device.

## Timeline
| s | what happens | where it lives |
|---|---|---|
| 0.0–0.5 | the doorway establishes; nobody moves | blocked |
| 0.5–3.8 | crosses from the door to the console, 5.55 m at about 1.6 m/s | blocked (path, pace) · acted (a relaxed walk) |
| 3.8–4.5 | slows and stands at the console, facing the device | blocked (the stop) · acted (the last step) |
| 4.5–5.5 | raises the left hand and presses the button | **acted only** — the greybox shows the button dip at 5.35–5.5 s |
| 5.5–7.5 | the device brightens blue — **caused by the press, never before it** | blocked |
| 7.5–8.0 | camera and figure hold | blocked |
| 0.0–7.5 | the camera pushes in and settles | blocked |

## Camera
28 mm, starts low-left of the door looking at the console, pushes in about 3.2 m over 7.5 s, eases out, holds on a medium-wide frame with the whole device in shot.

## Triggers
Button pressed at 5.35–5.5 s → device glow from 5.5 s, rising over two seconds.

## Assumptions
8 s, 24 fps and 1280×720 are the project defaults — the user gave none. One person. The researcher uses the left hand (the side the camera sees).
