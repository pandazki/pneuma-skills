# Taste Profile

> 这是一份空白起步的口味档案。你不需要先描述风格；它只从真实写作任务里的选择、否决和亲手修改中生长。

## 0. Calibration

- Chinese long-form: uncalibrated
- Evidence-backed defaults come from the bundled `preset-default.md`.
- Other genres and languages remain uncalibrated.
